How it works

One QR code per group

  1. Select the clinical topic.
  2. Choose usual care or LeafAtlas leaflet.
  3. Generate and print the QR code.
  4. The parent scans it and goes directly to Question 1.

There are five topics and two study groups, giving ten distinct signed questionnaire links.

The study groups are independent. The questions are identical for both groups within each topic.
